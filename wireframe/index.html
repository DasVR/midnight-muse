Blind Date

Three ways it could work. Pick one.

Option A · Recommended

The Mystery Session

You book a date and a tier. Dani designs a secret set around you, and you see it when you walk in.

  1. Choose a date and tier from the calendar
  2. Answer five short questions about your taste
  3. Dani builds the set and styles you in secret
  4. The reveal happens on the day

Needs a quiz in the booking form

Option B

The Matchmaker

A short quiz matches you to one of Dani's aesthetics. You see your match before you book.

  1. Take a six-question quiz on the page
  2. Get matched to a set: Candlelit, Coquette, Y2K…
  3. See photos from that set and its price
  4. Book it, or retake the quiz

Needs a quiz page and a result per set

Option C

Sealed Envelopes

A row of sealed envelopes, each hiding a set and a price. You unlock one with the key and book what you get.

  1. Pick an envelope, blind
  2. Unlock it with the key (same motion as The Work)
  3. See the set, the tier and the price
  4. Book it within 48 hours, or it reseals

Needs a pool of ready-to-go sets

FAQ

Are travel expenses included?

In most cases around ½ an hour of travel time is included in your session, but there are extra fees for anything further. We can figure out exact costs when discussing location.

This is my first photoshoot, what should I know?

You will never have to be a model or know how to pose! I will always guide you through posing and do my best to make sure you're comfortable and having fun.

All nerves melted away so quickly.

JAYME
What if the weather is bad?

Florida is very unpredictable, and we can always reschedule for no extra charge if you're not comfortable going out in bad weather.

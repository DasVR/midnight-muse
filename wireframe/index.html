FAQ

Are travel expenses included?

In most cases around ½ an hour of travel time is included in your session, but there are extra fees for anything further. We can figure out exact costs when discussing location.

This is my first photoshoot, what should I know?

You will never have to be a model or know how to pose! I will always guide you through posing and do my best to make sure you're comfortable and having fun.

All nerves melted away so quickly.

JAYME
How many photos will I get?

It depends on the package. For portraits it runs from 5 to 10+ edited photos with a Mini up to 25+ with the Muse. Every package lists its count under Sessions.

What should I wear? Do you have outfits?

Bring what feels like you. For portraits, the Styled package adds outfit options, and Complete and Muse come with curated outfits and props, so you can leave the styling to me.

Can you build a set around my idea?

That's my favourite kind of shoot. Send your idea or a Pinterest board with your booking and I'll design the set around it. The Doll Houses are a few I've built before.

Do you do maternity sessions?

Yes! Maternity is a tier under Couples, shot like a couples session: the two of you, golden light, somewhere green. It's about you as a family before the baby arrives, not the birth itself.

How do I book, and is there a deposit?

Check which days I'm free under Open Dates, then send me a letter through the form below with the days that suit you. I'll write back to confirm. Deposit and payment details to come from Dani.

When will I get my photos?

Your edited photos arrive within 1 to 2 weeks of your session.

What if the weather is bad?

Florida is very unpredictable, and we can always reschedule for no extra charge if you're not comfortable going out in bad weather.
